Karthik Sreedhar

PhD Candidate in Computer Science

I am a 3rd year PhD student at Columbia University. My research focuses are building human AI systems to empower productivity and creativity as well as running simulations with multi-agent LLM systems.

Currently, I am working on a project to build a human-AI system to enable end users to iteratively customize their email workflows by authoring new interface features and processes through conversational interaction.

I am advised by Professor Lydia Chilton and is part of the Data, Processing, & Agents Lab (DAPLAB) and the Computational Design Lab.
