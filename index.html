PhD Candidate in Computer Science

Karthik Sreedhar

I am a 3rd year PhD student at Columbia University. My research focuses are building human-AI systems to empower productivity and creativity, as well as running simulations with multi-agent LLM systems.

I am currently a visiting researcher at the MIT Media Lab's Camera Culture group, where I work with Professor Ramesh Raskar on AI agent inboxes. My current focus there is whether personal AI agents can surface latent consumer demand without exposing what any individual user wants. I am also building malleable systems: human-AI systems that let end users iteratively customize their email workflows by authoring new interface features and processes through conversational interaction.

I am advised by Professor Lydia Chilton and am part of the Data, Processing, & Agents Lab (DAPLAB) and the Computational Design Lab.
