Karthik Sreedhar

PhD Candidate in Computer Science

I am a 3rd year PhD student at Columbia University. My research focuses are building human AI systems to empower productivity and creativity as well as running simulations with multi-agent LLM systems.

I am currently a visiting researcher at the MIT Media Lab's Camera Culture group, working with Professor Ramesh Raskar on whether personal AI agents can surface latent consumer demand without exposing what any individual user wants. I am also building a human-AI system that lets end users iteratively customize their email workflows by authoring new interface features and processes through conversational interaction.

I am advised by Professor Lydia Chilton and am part of the Data, Processing, & Agents Lab (DAPLAB) and the Computational Design Lab.

Selected Work

Simulating Cooperative Prosocial Behavior with Multi-Agent LLMs: Evidence and Mechanisms for AI Agents to Inform Policy Decisions

Demonstrates that multi-agent LLM systems can realistically simulate human prosocial behavior-both replicating controlled lab experiments and generating complex, real-world interactions-suggesting their potential as tools for exploring and informing policy decisions.
